How support works

Practical, respectful and responsive.

Counselling may involve understanding patterns and triggers, exploring motivation, practising coping skills, preparing for difficult situations and reviewing what supports ongoing progress. The focus is collaborative and individualised.

Shree brings relevant experience in AOD program facilitation, psychoeducation, individual support and harm-reduction approaches, alongside broader mental health and healthcare experience.

Group counselling: Group counselling and family support sessions may also be arranged based on individual needs, suitability and demand. Please enquire for further information.

Service boundaries

Know where to seek urgent help.

Clarvia is not an emergency service, inpatient detoxification program or medically supervised withdrawal service. Alcohol or drug withdrawal can involve serious medical risks and may require assessment by an appropriate medical professional.

Immediate danger or emergency: Call 000. For crisis support in Australia, call Lifeline on 13 11 14. Do not use email or unattended forms for urgent support.
