Meet your guide

A warm, reflective space to reconnect with your inner voice.

Welcome to Clarvia Wellness. I’m Shree, a psychology trainee and healthcare professional with a purpose-led approach to emotional wellbeing. Clarvia Wellness was born from a calling to create gentle, reflective spaces for those seeking emotional clarity, peace, and a return to their inner voice.

Having supported individuals through both clinical and holistic lenses, I hold space for you not as an expert, but as a companion on your path back to self. These sessions are for those feeling emotionally overwhelmed, caught in cycles of overthinking, or yearning to be guided by their heart.
