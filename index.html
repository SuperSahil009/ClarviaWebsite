Common questions

A few things you might be wondering.

Do I need to know exactly what I want to discuss?

No. It is enough to know that something has been difficult or that you would value a space to reflect. We can begin by making sense of what feels most important now.

Are sessions available online?

Yes. Sessions are predominantly online, making support accessible from an appropriate private space across Australia. Selected mobile visits in Melbourne may be available by arrangement.

How much do sessions cost?

A free introductory call is 30 minutes. Individual counselling is $150 AUD for 60 minutes. A three-session package is $400 AUD; please enquire while its booking and payment workflow is being confirmed.

Is what I share confidential?

Your privacy is taken seriously, but confidentiality is not absolute. Limits can apply where there is a serious risk of harm, a legal requirement to disclose, or another authorised exception. These limits are discussed during consent.

Can I have an introductory conversation first?

Yes. The free 30-minute introductory call is a chance to discuss what you are looking for and whether Clarvia may be a suitable fit. There is no obligation to continue.
