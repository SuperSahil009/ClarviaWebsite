The Clarvia approach

Trauma happens in layers. And healing does too.

Sometimes what we carry comes from experiences we remember. Sometimes it's shaped by our family, environment, relationships, emotions, or beliefs about ourselves. Understanding these influences can help us respond with compassion and explore ways forward.

Explore the layers

What we carry can have many influences.

Choose a layer to learn more about how it may shape our experiences and what compassionate support might involve.

01Generational & ancestral

Family experiences and learned patterns can influence how we respond to stress, safety and connection.

How we can respond: Explore inherited expectations and patterns with curiosity, while recognising your own choices.

02Environment & upbringing

Culture, community, resources and the circumstances we grow up in can affect how safe or supported we feel.

How we can respond: Notice how environments shape coping and consider the supports available now.

03Relationships & attachment

Experiences with family, caregivers and partners can influence trust, boundaries and relationships.

How we can respond: Reflect on relational patterns, communication and what supportive connection means to you.

04Emotions & experiences

Difficult experiences can leave us with strong feelings, protective responses or emotions that are hard to name.

How we can respond: Build emotional awareness and compassionate ways to respond to difficult feelings.

05Thoughts & beliefs

Messages we've absorbed can become assumptions about ourselves, others and the world.

How we can respond: Explore self-talk and beliefs and practise more flexible, helpful perspectives.

06Body & stress responses

Stress and adversity can affect sleep, tension, alertness, and how our bodies respond to reminders.

How we can respond: Try comfortable, optional grounding and regulation practices; seek specialist support when appropriate.

These are overlapping influences, not six established trauma stages or a diagnosis. Everyone's experience is different; healing doesn't require addressing every layer in sequence.

Common questions

Before you reach out.

Prefer to ask something privately? Email Clarvia.

Do I need to know exactly what I want to discuss?

No. We can begin with what feels important now.

Are sessions available online?

Yes. Sessions are predominantly online across appropriate Australian service areas.

How much do sessions cost?

The introductory call is free; general counselling is $135 AUD for 60 minutes, AOD counselling is $160 AUD, and a four-week personalised support package costs $500 AUD. See booking options for the latest details.

Is what I share confidential?

Privacy is taken seriously, but confidentiality has limits where there is serious risk, a legal requirement or another authorised exception. These limits are discussed during consent.
