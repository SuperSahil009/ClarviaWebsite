Meet your guide

A journey shaped by change, compassion and understanding.

Hi, I'm Shree, the person behind Clarvia Wellness.

At 19, I moved to Australia, stepping into a new country, a new culture and a life filled with uncertainty. Along the way, I experienced loneliness, anxiety and the challenges of finding my feet while building a life away from everything familiar.

My journey taught me that personal growth isn't always straightforward. Through mindfulness, self-reflection and learning practical ways to navigate difficult emotions, I began developing a deeper understanding of myself and what it means to find balance through life's changes.

Alongside my personal experiences, I've spent more than five years working in healthcare, including mental health and alcohol and other drug services, and completed my Psychology Honours studies. These experiences have shaped the compassionate, grounded and practical approach I bring to Clarvia.

My journey has taught me that healing isn't always linear. Sometimes it begins with being heard, sometimes with understanding ourselves, and sometimes with taking one small step forward.

The Clarvia approach

Healing happens in layers.

Sometimes we need to talk things through. Sometimes we need to understand our patterns, reconnect with our bodies or find practical strategies for everyday life. There isn't one approach that works for everyone.

01

Feeling heard and understood

Compassionate conversation, connection and room to explore experiences without judgement.

02

Understanding your patterns

Exploring thoughts, emotions, triggers, habits and the experiences that influence your responses.

03

Finding moments of calm

Optional mindfulness, gentle breathing, grounding and body awareness, adapted to your comfort.

04

Building practical strategies

Developing coping skills, boundaries, healthier routines and everyday tools.

05

Making meaningful changes

Taking manageable steps, reviewing what works and adjusting support as life evolves.

Common questions

Before you reach out.

Prefer to ask something privately? Email Clarvia.

Do I need to know exactly what I want to discuss?

No. We can begin with what feels important now.

Are sessions available online?

Yes. Sessions are predominantly online across appropriate Australian service areas.

How much do sessions cost?

The introductory call is free. Individual counselling is $150 AUD for 60 minutes. A three-session package is $400 AUD; please enquire while its booking workflow is being confirmed.

Is what I share confidential?

Privacy is taken seriously, but confidentiality has limits where there is serious risk, a legal requirement or another authorised exception. These limits are discussed during consent.
